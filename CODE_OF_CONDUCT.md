# Code of Conduct

[Tiếng Việt](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.vi.md) · **English**

## Our Standard

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for all contributors and participants in the MeetScribe project.

We expect everyone who contributes to issues, discussions, documentation, or code to interact with empathy, courtesy, and constructive intent, regardless of technical background, experience level, language proficiency, or personal identity.

---

## Expected Behavior

- **Be Respectful & Empathetic:** Engage constructively with differing perspectives and maintain professional courtesy.
- **Focus on the Work:** Keep discussions focused on technical improvements, user privacy, documentation accuracy, and codebase quality.
- **Support Bilingual Contributions:** MeetScribe welcomes contributions in both English and Vietnamese. Be patient with non-native speakers of either language.
- **Gracefully Accept Constructive Feedback:** View code reviews and technical critiques as opportunities to improve the project.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting remarks, or inflammatory rhetoric.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Unsolicited commercial advertising or spam.

---

## Reporting & Inquiries

If you experience or witness behavior that violates these principles, please raise the matter with the project maintainers via a GitHub issue; ask for a private channel before sharing sensitive details. Maintainers will review reports in good faith to maintain a productive, respectful collaboration space.
